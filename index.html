Insights — MAY 1 – JUL 31, 2026 analysis
01

Conversation Engine

The Emgrand influencer ads “NOOR.ZL1” and “Mohamed Marhoon” brought in 2,532 conversations (42% of messaging-campaign conversations) by spending only $474 — $0.19 each against a $0.52 average.

02

Reach Driver

“Driving Baghdad — Carousel” captured $760.51 (9% of spend) and 3.28M impressions at a $0.23 CPM with 47,356 clicks — the backbone of reach.

03

Coolray — Higher Cost per Conversation

Coolray messaging campaigns cost $0.88 per conversation against $0.34 for Emgrand on near-equal spend ($1,283 vs $1,261); excluding influencer ads the gap is $0.94 vs $0.65.
